# Privacy Policy for NailBiteBreaker

**Effective Date: October 26, 2023**

NailBiteBreaker is built as a Commercial app. This SERVICE is provided by the developer at no cost and is intended for use as is. This page is used to inform visitors regarding our policies with the collection, use, and disclosure of Personal Information if anyone decided to use our Service.

## 1. No Data Collection (Privacy by Design)
NailBiteBreaker is designed to be completely private. 
*   **No Account Required**: You do not need to create an account to use the app.
*   **Local Storage Only**: All data you enter (urge logs, habit stats, coaching progress) is stored **locally on your device** using an encrypted-at-rest-ready Android Room database.
*   **No Cloud Sync**: We do not transmit, collect, or store your personal data on any external servers or cloud services.

## 2. Children’s Privacy
Our Service does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13 years of age. Since all data is stored locally and we do not collect any personal information, we do not have a way to identify the age of the user. If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so that we will be able to do necessary actions.

## 3. Third-Party Services
The app does not use third-party services that may collect information used to identify you.

## 4. Permissions
*   **VIBRATE**: Required only for haptic feedback during game interactions and logging.

## 5. Data Deletion and Security
Since all data is local, you have full control over your information:
*   **Uninstalling**: Uninstalling the app from your device will immediately and permanently delete all stored data, including your logs, streaks, and progress.
*   **Manual Reset**: You can clear all data by navigating to Android Settings > Apps > NailBiteBreaker > Storage and selecting "Clear Data".
*   **Security**: While we do not transmit data, we recommend using device-level security (PIN, pattern, or biometrics) to protect the information stored on your phone.

## 6. Contact Us
If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us.
